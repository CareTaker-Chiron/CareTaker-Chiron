# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Caretaker Chiron a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different perspectives and experience levels.
- Give and accept feedback professionally.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.
- Respect people's time, privacy, and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insults, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or other inappropriate conduct.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Reports should be sent privately to the maintainers.

This policy is based on the Contributor Covenant.
