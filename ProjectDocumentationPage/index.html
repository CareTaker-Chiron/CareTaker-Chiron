Understand your system. Explore beneath the surface.

Caretaker Chiron is an open-source system exploration platform designed to make the inner workings of your computer understandable, observable, and accessible — without sacrificing technical depth.

Explore on GitHub Discover the vision

Your computer is doing more than you can see.

Operating systems expose an enormous amount of useful information: which programs are running, what they consume, how they relate to one another. Most of it is hard to read unless you already know what you are looking at.

Advanced utilities such as Process Hacker and x64dbg reveal remarkable detail, but they assume expertise. Simpler monitors are approachable, but often stop at the surface.

Caretaker Chiron is an attempt to bridge that gap: make the complex understandable, without making the underlying technology less powerful.

Powerful enough to investigate. Clear enough to understand.

  • Clarity without oversimplification

    Complex information should be understandable without removing the technical details that make it useful.

  • Evidence before explanations

    System explanations should be grounded in observable evidence, not unsupported guesses.

  • Local-first and privacy-conscious

    Prefer local inspection and transparent data handling. Cloud assistance should be optional.

  • User control and safety

    Privileged operations should be explicit. AI must not silently execute arbitrary system commands.

Building it one layer at a time.

The project is in early development. Stages describe intent and may change.

  1. 01

    Foundation

    Repository, architecture, application shell, platform detection, IPC.

  2. 02

    Native system intelligence

    Process enumeration, resource monitoring, process inspection, search and sorting.

  3. 03

    Investigation experience

    Evidence collection, system visualization, technical explanations.

  4. 04

    AI assistance

    Evidence-grounded explanations, local models, optional cloud support.

  5. 05

    Cross-platform maturity

    Windows, Linux, macOS validation, testing, packaging, releases.
